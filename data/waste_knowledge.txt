# SustainAI local knowledge base. General information only; local rules take priority.

[Plastic]
Reduce single-use plastic and prefer reusable alternatives. Empty and clean accepted containers where practical. Not all plastic packaging is accepted everywhere.

[Paper and Cardboard]
Keep paper and cardboard dry and reasonably clean. Reuse boxes before recycling. Food-soiled material may need another route depending on local guidance.

[Glass]
Bottles and jars may be collected separately. Handle broken glass carefully. Ceramics and heat-resistant cookware may have different requirements.

[Metal]
Aluminium and steel containers are often recyclable. Empty and rinse them where practical. Recycling helps preserve mineral resources.

[Organic waste]
Food scraps and fruit or vegetable peels can often be composted or collected as organic waste. Keep organics separate from dry recyclables.

[E-waste and Electronics]
Phones, laptops, chargers, headphones, and cables should not normally go into ordinary household bins. Use an authorized e-waste collection or take-back option where available.

[Batteries]
Keep batteries out of ordinary waste. Do not burn, crush, open, or mix them. Follow local battery collection guidance.

[Textiles]
Repair, share, donate, or reuse clothing before disposal. Verify what local textile programs accept.

[Hazardous waste]
Paints, solvents, pesticides, and strong chemicals should not be poured into drains, burned, or mixed with normal waste. Follow local hazardous-waste instructions.

[Reduction and Reuse]
Prevent waste by buying only what is needed, choosing durable products, repairing items, and selecting reusable options.

[Recycling and Composting]
Recycling works best when accepted materials are sorted correctly and free of contamination. Composting turns suitable organic material into a soil amendment. Local acceptance rules vary.

[Responsible consumption]
Consider durability, repairability, packaging, and end-of-life options before buying.
