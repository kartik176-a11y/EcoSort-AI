# Hazardous and Special Waste Guidance

[Overview]
Hazardous waste includes materials that are toxic, corrosive, flammable, or reactive. These require special handling and must NOT go into regular household waste.

[Common Hazardous Items]

**Household Chemicals:**
- Paints and paint thinners
- Varnishes and wood stains
- Cleaning solvents
- Pesticides and herbicides
- Pool chemicals
- Automotive fluids (motor oil, antifreeze)

**Medical Waste:**
- Expired medications
- Needles and syringes
- Medical sharps

**Other Hazardous Items:**
- Batteries (especially lead-acid car batteries)
- Fluorescent bulbs and CFLs (contain mercury)
- Aerosol cans with contents
- Fire extinguishers

[Proper Disposal]

**DO NOT:**
- Pour chemicals down drains or toilets
- Mix different chemicals
- Burn hazardous waste
- Place in regular trash bins
- Bury in soil

**DO:**
- Store safely in original containers
- Keep away from children and pets
- Contact local authorities for collection programs
- Use authorized hazardous waste collection facilities
- Check for take-back programs (e.g., pharmacy medication returns)

[Why Special Handling?]
Hazardous waste can:
- Contaminate soil and groundwater
- Harm sanitation workers
- Cause fires in waste facilities
- Release toxic fumes
- Harm wildlife and ecosystems

[Vizag Context]
Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation (GVMC) may organize special collection drives for hazardous waste. Contact GVMC or local municipal office for:
- Collection schedules
- Authorized drop-off locations
- Safe disposal guidance

[Batteries]
Used batteries (alkaline, rechargeable, lithium) should never go in regular trash. Many retailers and electronics stores have battery collection boxes.

[Medications]
Return expired or unused medications to pharmacies that accept them. Do not flush down toilet or throw in regular trash.

[Automotive]
Motor oil, antifreeze, brake fluid should be taken to authorized collection centers or auto service centers that accept used fluids.

[Important]
When in doubt, contact your local municipal authority or environmental office. It is better to ask than to dispose improperly.
