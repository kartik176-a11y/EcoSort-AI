[GVMC Green City Action Plan theme]
Waste segregation at the household level is important for reducing contamination in organic and recyclable flows. Wet waste includes food leftovers, vegetable scraps, and fruit peels. Dry waste includes cardboard, paper, plastic, and glass. Special waste includes battery, electronic, and chemical waste.

[GVMC municipal waste guidance]
Households should keep wet waste separate from dry waste to support composting and recycling. Items like used batteries and electronics should not be mixed with regular domestic waste and should be routed to designated collection or authorized disposal systems.

[Local municipal reference]
For special or uncertain waste streams, households should consult local municipal authorities or authorized waste collection points before disposal.

[Waste handling reminder]
Do not burn, dump, or mix hazardous waste with regular household garbage. Always verify the final route with the local authority when unsure.
