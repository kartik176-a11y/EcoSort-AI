[Swachh Bharat Mission guidance]
Recyclables such as plastic bottles, bottles, newspapers, and cardboard should be stored separately from food waste. Organic waste should be directed to compost or wet-waste channels. Hazardous waste such as used batteries, paints, and electronic items must be treated through special collection mechanisms.

[Household segregation rules]
Wet or biodegradable waste should be separated from dry and recyclable waste. Household members should avoid mixing used batteries or electronic items with regular waste. Municipal rules and authorized collection points should be checked for special waste items.

[General disposal principle]
Waste should be disposed of according to the category and local guidance. When the item is uncertain or potentially hazardous, verify with the local authority before disposal.
