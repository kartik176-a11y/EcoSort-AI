# General Waste Guidance and Uncertain Items

[When Classification is Uncertain]
If you are not sure how to classify a waste item:
1. Do NOT guess
2. Check with your local municipal office
3. Ask sanitation workers during collection
4. Refer to official GVMC or Swachh Bharat guidance
5. When in doubt, keep it separate and verify before disposal

[Mixed or Composite Materials]
Some items contain multiple materials (e.g., tetra packs, laminated packaging, multi-layer plastics). These may have special requirements:
- Check local acceptance lists
- Contact manufacturer for guidance
- Verify with municipal collection program

[Non-Recyclable Items]
Certain items are generally not recyclable in most programs:
- Severely food-soiled paper/cardboard
- Broken ceramics and pottery
- Mirrors and window glass
- Styrofoam (in many areas)
- Wax-coated paper
- Carbon paper
- Thermal paper receipts (in some programs)

These may go to general waste or require special disposal. Check locally.

[Bulky Waste]
Large items like furniture, mattresses, carpets:
- Contact municipality for bulk waste collection
- Schedule special pickup
- Use authorized disposal facilities
- Consider donation or resale if still usable

[Construction and Demolition Waste]
Building materials, rubble, concrete:
- Requires separate disposal channel
- Contact local construction waste management facility
- Do not mix with household waste

[Textiles and Clothing]
- Donate usable clothing to charities
- Repair before discarding
- Check for textile recycling programs
- Some municipalities have separate textile collection

[Organic Garden Waste]
Grass clippings, leaves, branches:
- Compost at home if possible
- Check for green waste collection programs
- Keep separate from food waste in some programs

[General Principles]

**Source Segregation:**
Separate waste at home into appropriate categories. This is the foundation of effective waste management.

**Reduce First:**
Before disposal, consider:
- Can it be avoided?
- Can it be reused?
- Can it be repaired?
- Can it be donated?

**Clean and Dry:**
Keep recyclables clean and dry. Contaminated materials may not be accepted.

**Follow Local Rules:**
Waste management rules vary by location. Always follow your local municipal guidelines.

[Responsible Consumption]
Make sustainable choices:
- Choose products with less packaging
- Prefer reusable over single-use
- Buy durable, repairable items
- Support local repair services
- Choose products from companies with take-back programs

[Contact Information]
For Visakhapatnam residents:
- Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation (GVMC)
- Local ward office
- Swachh Bharat helpline
- Municipal sanitation department

[Important Reminder]
This guidance is general and informational. Official rules from your local municipal authority always take priority. When uncertain, verify before disposal.
